# Day 3 retrospective (Asakusa → Skytree Town, 2026-05-31) — feed for propose-itinerary

Source: `journals/day-3-2026-05-31.md` (full Albee captures). Use alongside [[travel-patterns]]. This was the **best day of the trip** — and it got that way by doing *less*.

## What worked
- **One anchor + huge white space.** Packed plan (Senso-ji → Kappabashi → Skytree deck → aquarium → Ikebukuro Pokémon → Sky Circus) got ~1/3 done; the unplanned hours were the highlights. "This is the nicest part of my day" / "I like the slow travel, I just want to sit in one spot."
- **Dropping whole legs cost nothing.** Skipped the Skytree deck, the aquarium, and the entire Ikebukuro evening — nobody minded.
- **Pokémon GO turned the walk to Skytree into a game** ("they forget they're walking").
- **Make-your-own onigiri** beat any sukiyaki institution — interactive food wins.
- **A home-base rest spot** (riverside playground, then a hammock + cloud-watching) carried hours of contentment.
- **Rotating the adults** ("an adult has to be happy too; no one's happy if any one of us isn't").

## What didn't work
- **Dinner failed** — Candice: "nothing I'd want" (pork/ramen problem). No Candice-safe option pre-locked.
- **The Suica card situation** — three work, one dead; left unresolved.

## Kid energy + behavior patterns
- **Mornings need meltdown margin** — Kai's morning explosion + recurring Zara bathroom friction. Nothing tight before ~10:00.
- **Emotional coaching lands** — the shame/guilt reframe ("those are good feelings, because you're a good person") reset Kai.
- **Toys are regulation tools** — the Charizard bottle + new Pokémon toys carried hours of cooperative play.

## Grandma (Popo) + Candice observations
- **Candice flagged she might be getting sick** ("afraid Momo's getting sick"). Watch energy.

## Takeaways for upcoming days
- Plan ONE must-do, protect big unscheduled blocks; it's fine to drop legs.
- Pre-lock a Candice-safe dinner in the morning (non-negotiable).
- Have a walking game ready for any stretch >10 min.
- Bias meals toward DIY/interactive over famous institutions.
