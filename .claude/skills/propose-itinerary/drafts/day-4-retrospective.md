# Day 4 retrospective (Odaiba, 2026-06-01) — feed for propose-itinerary

Source: `journals/day-4-2026-06-01.md` (full Albee captures, 274 moments). Use alongside [[travel-patterns]]. This was the **hardest day of the trip** — and the clearest lesson in what breaks the family and what repairs it.

## What worked
- **A deliberate generous act reset the whole day.** The secret Labubu mission — buying Zara the toy and framing *Kai* as the giver — flipped a morning of fighting. "When you're generous, I want to be generous."
- **teamLab (interactive/AR) was the runaway hit** — the catch-the-animals forest ("I'm just into catching now") and the water room beat every static exhibit.
- **The sushi-counter dinner** (Ginza Six, Itamae) was the best meal of the trip after a near-collapse — à-la-carte sushi + shrimp tempura, ~$80 for five.
- **Pokémon GO carried the day** — the bay walk, the museum walks, and especially the night walk home, on a cooperative turn-taking rule ("three catches each, hold hands, switch").

## What didn't work
- **The anchor was locked.** Pokémon GO Fest turned out ticketed; the one thing the day was built on fell out before 10am, with no white-space fallback. (Direct contrast to Day 3.)
- **Rush-hour train crush genuinely scared the kids** ("I want to go home"). → never travel peak.
- **Dinner nearly collapsed** ("I give up, I don't know how to solve this") before the sushi save — no Candice-safe dinner pre-locked, again.
- **ATM / dead-IC-card / cash friction** ate real time ("this is adding a lot of friction to our trip").

## Kid energy + behavior patterns
- **Sibling competition is the day's real tax** — it drove most of the hard moments. Fixes that worked: separate the kids between adults, give each a *job* (navigator, phone-holder), rotate — *early*, not after it blows up.
- **Hunger/tiredness = meltdown** — feed before the crash; hold dessert as a tool, not a bribe.
- **Hard parenting honesty** — Ajay needed "a total reset"; modeling repair ("I was stressed, I'm okay now") mattered more than discipline.

## Grandma (Popo) + Candice observations
- **Popo is sick** (confirmed: "Popo is sick, Kai") — sat out stretches, rested in shade. Build genuinely easy days around her.

## Takeaways for upcoming days
- Never schedule travel during rush hour.
- Don't anchor a day on one ticketed/uncertain thing without a fallback.
- Pre-lock a Candice-safe dinner each morning — **sushi/tempura is the proven format.**
- When competition spirals, reach for a generous act / separate-and-rotate, not more correction.
- Bias toward interactive, body-in-it experiences (teamLab > museums-you-look-at).
