# Day 7 — Kyoto arrival (Thu Jun 4) — proposal v2

*v2 redirect: drop Nijo Castle; do one walkable Gion/Higashiyama district loop near the hotel — scenery + gardens + geisha + street food, all flat, no train.*

## Candice-voice summary

Babes — scrap the train to Nijo. We land at 12:12 and Stitch is a 5-minute walk from the river, so Day 1 is **one beautiful loop through Gion on foot** — no transit, all flat, easy on Popo. Drop bags, graze street food at **Nishiki Market**, then cross Shijo Bridge and wander: the **Shirakawa Canal** (the prettiest willow-lined lane in the city), down **Hanamikoji-dori**, into **Kennin-ji** for our garden + the Twin Dragons ceiling (kids will actually like that one), up the lanes to **Kodai-ji**'s hillside garden if we've still got legs, then **Yasaka Shrine** and Maruyama Park. We time it so we're back on **Hanamikoji at dusk (~5:45–6:15)** for the maiko window — look, don't chase — and end at **Pontocho** for dinner. Two musts the second we arrive: have Stitch **book Pontocho** (your P1) and confirm the Hakone bags landed. Gardens close ~5, so we front-load them and let the evening be Gion + dinner.

## The loop (south Higashiyama / Gion — all walking)
- **12:12** Kyoto Station → Stitch (~12:35). Drop bags (formal check-in 3pm; front desk holds; you can check in anytime after).
- **At the desk, immediately:** ① **book Pontocho dinner** (~18:30, Candice P1), ② confirm takkyubin from Hakone arrived.
- **~12:45–13:45 Lunch — Nishiki Market** (5 min, west side). This is the **street-food grazing** stop: skewers, tamago, tako-tamago, soy-milk donuts, pickles. Graze, don't commit.
- **~13:45 cross Shijo Bridge** into Gion.
- **~14:00 Shirakawa Canal** — willow-lined, stone-paved, the prettiest scenery in Gion. Photos.
- **~14:20 Hanamikoji-dori** — the classic Gion teahouse street (quieter by day; we return at dusk for maiko).
- **~14:40–15:40 Kennin-ji** (south end of Hanamikoji) — oldest Zen temple, **Twin Dragons ceiling** + ○△□ garden. ¥800 adult. *Last entry 16:30.* The kid-friendly garden pick.
- **~15:50–16:45 Kodai-ji** *(optional — drop if energy fades)* — hillside Zen garden, bamboo path, via the Nene-no-michi lane. ¥600. *Last entry 17:00.* (Walk up through **Ishibei-koji**, a gorgeous preserved stone alley, on the way.)
- **~16:45 Yasaka Shrine + Maruyama Park** — free, vermilion lanterns; **Imo Pi-pi** purple-potato ice cream nearby (P3 treat).
- **~17:30 back down to Hanamikoji for the maiko window (~17:45–18:15)** — dusk, as they move between teahouses. Etiquette: no photos without permission, don't follow.
- **~18:30 Pontocho dinner** (P1, booked). Candice-safe counter: chicken/seafood/sukiyaki, not pork-only. Fallbacks: 料理屋 てら戸 (sushi, 8 seats — needs booking), Men-ya Inoichi (ramen).
- **After:** Kamo River stroll / dessert; optional adults-only L'Escamoteur if Popo takes the kids back.

## Kid-energy reality (decision #7)
- Two gardens + temples back-to-back risks the "contemplative temple" boredom. **Kennin-ji is the keeper** (dragons ceiling = the hook). **Kodai-ji is the droppable one** — cut it if the kids are fading and go straight Yasaka → dusk → dinner. Toys come along as regulation tools.
