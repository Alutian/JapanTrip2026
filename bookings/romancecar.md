## ⚠️ You Still Need a Base Fare Ticket

The ¥4,610 above is the **Limited Express surcharge only**. To board you also need a **base fare ticket** covering the Shinjuku → Hakone-Yumoto distance on the Odakyu line. Three ways:

1. **PASMO/Suica IC card** — tap in at Shinjuku, tap out at Hakone-Yumoto. ~¥1,260/adult, ~¥630/kid auto-charged. Easiest.
2. **Paper ticket** — buy at Shinjuku machines before boarding.
3. **Hakone Free Pass — buy at Shinjuku** ⭐ recommended. Includes round-trip Shinjuku ↔ Hakone base fare AND covers all Hakone Tozan / Ropeway / boats / buses for 2 days. ~¥6,100/adult + ~¥1,100/kid ≈ ¥21,500. Buy at the **Odakyu Sightseeing Service Center** near the Romancecar platform on Jun 2 before boarding. (Replaces the previous plan to buy at Yumoto on arrival.)
